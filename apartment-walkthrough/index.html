Controls

  • Mouse look around
  • WASD or arrows: walk
  • Shift walk faster
  • ←→ turn without the mouse
  • E or F use what the dot is on, for example a lamp, the TV, a window or a door
  • Click does the same while the mouse is captured
  • M floor plan on or off
  • [] darker or brighter
  • P still mode (path traced) on or off
  • Esc this menu
  • Drag on the left to walk; push to the edge to walk faster
  • Drag anywhere else to look around
  • Near a lamp, the TV, a window or a door, tap the button at the bottom
  • Menu, top right: this card
  • Hold the phone sideways for the widest view
