Controls

  • Mouse look around
  • WASD or arrows: walk
  • Shift walk faster
  • ←→ turn without the mouse
  • E open or close the nearest door
  • M floor plan on or off
  • [] darker or brighter
  • Esc this menu
  • Drag on the left to walk; push to the edge to walk faster
  • Drag anywhere else to look around
  • Near a door, tap the door button
  • Menu, top right: this card
  • Hold the phone sideways for the widest view
